01 / Our vision

Medical intelligence.
Within reach.

MaxMAI’s vision is to make advanced medical intelligence accessible across institutions and care settings.

We are building AutoMAX, an efficient platform that combines medical foundation models and automated, task-specific optimization to help hospitals, health systems, research institutions, and developers build and adapt medical AI to their own data and needs—with less compute and development effort.

Our first clinical focus is cardiac intelligence, with the goal of developing FDA-cleared decision-support tools for hospitals. Through integration with point-of-care ultrasound, we aim to extend these capabilities to rural and underserved communities with limited access to specialists.

04 / Our approach

Better models start
with better objectives.

Medical AI needs to work on the cases that are rare, ambiguous, and easy to miss. Our work starts with how models learn.

01

Train more efficiently.

We develop algorithms that reduce the cost of foundation-model training. Our earlier research outperformed OpenAI CLIP on evaluated benchmarks using 1/17 the compute and 1/10 the training samples processed.

02

Optimize what matters.

We develop objectives around AUROC, AUPRC, and partial AUC to address class imbalance and clinically relevant operating ranges.

03

Make adaptation practical.

Compact models and focused fine-tuning create a path toward deployment within the constraints of healthcare.
